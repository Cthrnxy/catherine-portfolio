About me

I'm a UI/UX designer who draws from everyday observations and frontline service experience to identify real user needs and turn them into clear, practical digital experiences.

My work is often inspired by real situations I've encountered where confusing information, inefficient processes, or small usability issues create unnecessary friction. I use those experiences as a starting point to explore how design can make services easier to understand and use.
